    Case study: automated liquidity reporting

    FRB 2052a (6G and LCR) reports at Bank of America.

    Pain point
    Manual data adjustments risked missing the daily 3pm EST cutoff, and late or rejected submissions carry fines.
    What I built
    Automation that validates non-automated data adjustments and applies FRB-specific codes so reports pass checklists before submission.
    Result
    Fewer manual adjustments and better on-time submission.
